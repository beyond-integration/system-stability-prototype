# Limitations

## Scientific scope

This prototype is a feasibility workflow for screening compact multi-planet systems. It is not a universal long-term stability predictor for all exoplanet architectures.

## Model assumptions

- The analytical screening methods are strongest in near-circular, coplanar, compact systems.
- The Hill and AMD criteria do not, by themselves, prove long-term stability.
- Resonance proximity is a useful diagnostic but is not equivalent to measured libration.
- The finite-horizon numerical integrations are only a finite-time test of dynamical behaviour.

## Data limitations

- Observed exoplanet tables can contain incomplete or uncertain measurements.
- Some systems are outside the strict applicability domain of the screening assumptions.
- Raw archive data are retained without modification, but the processed layers may still inherit observational uncertainty.

## Decision limitations

- Decision outcomes are restricted to the project’s screening logic and should not be presented as definitive physical classifications.
- The framework is useful for triage and prioritisation, not a replacement for detailed case-by-case numerical studies.

## Reporting

All outputs are stored with their assumptions, warnings, and method provenance to make the risk trade-offs explicit and auditable.
